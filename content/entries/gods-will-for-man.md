1. God's will for man is for man to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth (1 Timothy 2:4; 1 John 5: 20).

2. God's will for man is to save and deliver man so that man will serve Him without fear, in holiness and righteousness all the days of his life (Luke 1: 74-75; Ephesians 1:4).

3. God's will for man is for man to prosper (Deuteronomy 30: 5).

4. God's will for man is for man to be holy - that is, man should abstain from sexual immorality (1 Thessalonians 4: 3-5, 7).

5. God's will for man is for man to seek Him and to do good (Psalm 53:2-3).

6. God's will for man is for man to serve with love (1 John 4: 11-18).

7. God will for man, is that man should not be lost but should be raised up on the last day (John 6:39).
