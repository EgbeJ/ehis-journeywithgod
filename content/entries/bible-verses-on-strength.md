1. "The Lord is my strength and song, And He has become my salvation; He is my God, and I will praise Him; My father's God, and I will exalt Him" (Exodus 15:2)(NKJV).
2. "The Lord is my rock and my fortress and my deliverer; The God of my strength, in whom I will trust; My shield and the horn of my salvation, My stronghold and my refuge; My Saviour, You save me from violence (2 Samuel 22:2-3)(NKJV).
3. "God is my strength and power, And He makes my way perfect. He makes my feet like the feet of deer, And sets me on my high places. He teaches my hands to make war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze(2 Samuel 22: 33-35) (NKJV).
4. For God has armed me with strength for the battle; and He has subdued under me those who rose against me(2 Samuel 22:40).
5. "Seek the Lord and His strength; Seek His face evermore!" (1 Chronicles 16:11)(NKJV).
6. Honour and majesty are before God; Strength and gladness are in His place (1 Chronicles 16:27).
7. Both riches and honour come from God, and He reigns over all. In His hand is power and might; In God's hand it is to make great and to give strength to all ( 1 Chronicles 29:12).
8. Psalm 18:1- "I will love You, O Lord, my strength"
9. Psalm 18: 2- "The Lord is my rock and my fortress and my deliverer; My God, my strength, in whom I will trust; My shield and the horn of my salvation, My stronghold."
10. Psalm 18: 32- "It is God who arms me with strength, and makes my way perfect."
11. Psalm 18: 39- "God has armed me with strength for the battle; and have subdued under me those who rose up against me (2 Samuel 22:40)."
12. Psalm 19:14- "Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in Your sight, O Lord, my strength and my Redeemer".
13. Psalm 20:6- "Now I know that the Lord saves His anointed; He will answer him from His holy heaven with the saving strength of His right hand."
14. Psalm 27: 1- "The Lord is my light and my salvation; Whom shall I fear? The Lord is the strength of my life; Of whom shall I be afraid?
15. Psalm 28:7-8- "The Lord is my strength and my shield; My heart trusted in Him, and I am helped; Therefore my heart greatly rejoices, and with my song I will praise Him. The Lord is their strength, And He is the saving refuge of His anointed.
16. "The Lord will give strength to His people; The Lord will bless His people with peace". Psalm 29:11
17. Psalm 31: 4- "Pull me out of the net which they have secretly laid for me, For you are my strength."
18. Psalm 37: 39- "But the salvation of the righteous is from the Lord; He is their strength in the time of trouble."
19. Psalm 46:1- "God is our refuge and strength, A very present help in trouble".
20. Psalm 59: 17- "To You, O my strength, I will sing praises; For God is my defense, My God of mercy".
21. Psalm 62: 7- "In God is my salvation and my glory; The rock of my strength, And my refuge, is in God".
22. Psalm 68: 28- "Your God has commanded your strength; Strengthen, O God , what You have done for us."
23. God is my Strength (Psalm 31:4; Isaiah 12: 2;    Habakuk 3: 19).
24. Isaiah 12: 2- "Behold, God is my Salvation, I will trust and not be afraid; 'For Yah, the Lord, is my strength and song; He also has become my Salvation."
25. Habakuk 3:19 - " The Lord God is my Strength; He will make my feet like deer's feet, And He will make me walk on my high hills."
