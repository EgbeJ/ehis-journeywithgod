1. "The Lord is my strength and song, And He has become my salvation; He is my God, and I will praise Him; My father's God, and I will exalt Him" (Exodus 15:2)(NKJV).

2. " The Lord is my rock and my fortress and my deliverer; The God of my strength, in whom I will trust; My shield and the horn of my salvation, My stronghold and my refuge; My Saviour, You save me from violence (2 Samuel 22:2-3)(NKJV).

3. "God is my strength and power, And He makes my way perfect.

He makes my feet like the feet of deer, And sets me on my high places.

He teaches my hands to make war, so that my arms can bend a bow of bronze(2 Samuel 22: 33-35) (NKJV).

4. For God has armed me with strength for the battle; and He has subdued under me those who rose against me(2 Samuel 22:40).
