  Psalm 91:14-16; NKJV

  "Because he has set his love upon Me, therefore I will deliver him; I will set him on high, because he has known My name.

  He shall call upon Me, and I will answer him; I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him and honor him.

* God will deliver those who love Him.
* God will protect them.
* God will answer them when they call upon Him.
* God will be with them when they are in trouble.
* God will deliver them and honour them.
* God will satisfy them with long life.
* God will show them His salvation.
