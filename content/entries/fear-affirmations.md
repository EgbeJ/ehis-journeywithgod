---
title: Fear Affirmations
date: 2026-09-07T19:03:00.000+01:00
mood: cloud
moodLabel: Thoughtful
excerpt: >-
  These affirmations can be said when afraid or anxious. These affirmations
  should help you replace fearful thoughts with thoughts of faith in God and
  confidence in yourself.


  Our response to fear as Christians is to trust and have faith in God.
layout: entry.njk
---
1. God has commanded me to be strong and courageous, and not to be afraid, worried, disappointed more upset for HE is with me wherever I go (Joshua 1: 9).

2. Be strong and of good courage (Joshua 1: 6; Deuteronomy 31:6).

3. Judges 6:16 - "And the Lord said to him, 'Surely I will be with you, and you shall defeat the Midianites as one man' ".

4. Psalms 3:3 - "But You, O Lord, are a shield for me, My glory and the one who lifts up my head."

5. Hebrews 13: 6 - "The LORD is my helper; I will not fear". 

6. God goes with me, He will not leave me nor forsake me (Deuteronomy 31:6)

7. I choose to replace my fears with trust in God. God wants me to trust Him and not to lean in my own understanding (Proverbs 3: 5).

8. 2 Timothy 1: 7 - "For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power, and of love, and if a sound mind".

9. Isaiah 43:1-3 - "Fear not, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by your name; You are Mine.

When you pass through the waters, I will be with you; And through the rivers, they shall not overflow you. When you walk through the fire, you shall not be burned, nor shall the flame scorch you.

For I am the Lord your God, They Holy one of Israel, your saviour."

10. Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil; For God is with me; His rod and staff, they comfort me (Psalms 34:4).

11. Psalm 27: 1 - "The Lord is my light and my salvation; Whom shall I fear? The Lord is the strength of my life; Of whom shall I be afraid?"
